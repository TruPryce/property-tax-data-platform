## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Decode only the approved 17-byte NUMERIC wrapper

The Collin adapter SHALL accept only a bytes value that is exactly 17 bytes long. Byte zero SHALL be `0x00` for a non-negative value or `0x01` for a negative value. The remaining sixteen bytes SHALL contain four unsigned 32-bit magnitude words, each encoded little-endian and ordered most-significant word first. The decoder SHALL reconstruct the integer magnitude from those words without treating the wrapper as raw Jet storage, Automation `DECIMAL`, OLE DB `DB_NUMERIC`, or any universal Access wire format.

Precision and scale SHALL be required external column metadata rather than values encoded in the wrapper. Precision SHALL be an integer from 1 through 28, scale SHALL be an integer from 0 through the declared precision, and the decoder SHALL construct the exact value as `Decimal(magnitude).scaleb(-scale)` with the approved sign. It MUST NOT convert through binary floating point, silently round, infer precision or scale, or coerce the exponent.

Canonical zero SHALL use sign `0x00` and sixteen zero magnitude bytes. A negative sign with zero magnitude SHALL fail with `negative_zero`. A magnitude containing more decimal digits than the declared precision SHALL fail with `numeric_precision_overflow`. For precision `p` and scale `s`, accepted values SHALL remain within `-(10**p - 1) * 10**-s` through `(10**p - 1) * 10**-s` inclusive.

#### Scenario: Decode an independent signed multiword vector
- **WHEN** the decoder receives an independently authored 17-byte negative multiword literal with valid precision and scale metadata
- **THEN** it returns the reviewed exact negative `Decimal`
- **THEN** the sign, byte order, word order, declared precision, and declared scale are preserved
- **THEN** no float conversion, rounding, or exponent coercion occurs

#### Scenario: Decode canonical zero
- **WHEN** the decoder receives sign `0x00`, sixteen zero magnitude bytes, and valid precision and scale metadata
- **THEN** it returns exact non-negative zero with the declared precision and scale

#### Scenario: Reject negative zero
- **WHEN** the decoder receives sign `0x01` with sixteen zero magnitude bytes
- **THEN** decoding fails deterministically with `negative_zero`
- **THEN** no `Decimal` or partial accepted record is produced

#### Scenario: Reject malformed physical input
- **WHEN** a NUMERIC input is not bytes or is not exactly 17 bytes long
- **THEN** decoding fails with `invalid_numeric_buffer`

#### Scenario: Reject unsupported sign metadata or overflow
- **WHEN** the sign byte is not `0x00` or `0x01`, precision is absent or outside 1 through 28, scale is absent or outside 0 through precision, or the magnitude exceeds the declared precision
- **THEN** decoding fails respectively with `invalid_numeric_sign`, `invalid_numeric_precision`, `invalid_numeric_scale`, or `numeric_precision_overflow`
- **THEN** the malformed non-null value never becomes `None`

### Requirement: Use independent synthetic NUMERIC fixtures

The adapter test corpus SHALL contain small, independently authored literal vectors for canonical zero, positive, negative, scaled, multiword, precision-boundary, 2025, 2026, and monetary values. Every vector SHALL record its literal bytes, external precision and scale, reviewed expected `Decimal`, provenance, and checksum. Expected bytes or values MUST NOT be generated by the decoder, a production reader, or an encoder or helper that mirrors the decoder under test.

All fixtures SHALL be synthetic, identity-free, redistribution-safe, and free of county bytes, county rows, Access databases, archives, owner or DBA values, mailing or situs addresses, and production appraisal data.

#### Scenario: Review fixture independence
- **WHEN** a maintainer inspects the committed fixture manifest and fixture source
- **THEN** every required vector class is represented by an explicit reviewed literal and exact expected `Decimal`
- **THEN** checksums and synthetic provenance are documented
- **THEN** no mirrored encoder, county artifact, production row, or Access runtime generated the vectors

### Requirement: Validate the exact bounded AD_Public structure

The Collin adapter SHALL accept exactly the table named `AD_Public`; a missing table SHALL fail with `missing_required_table`, and any additional or differently named table SHALL fail with `unexpected_table`. The required privacy-minimized projection SHALL contain exactly these required columns:

- identity and status: `prop_id`, `geo_id`, `property_status`;
- source years: `curr_val_yr`, `cert_val_yr`;
- current values: `curr_imprv_hstd_val`, `curr_imprv_non_hstd_val`, `curr_land_hstd_val`, `curr_land_non_hstd_val`, `curr_ag_use_val`, `curr_ag_market`, `curr_market`, `curr_ag_loss`, `curr_appraised_val`, `curr_ten_percent_cap`, `curr_assessed_val`;
- certified values: `cert_imprv_hstd_val`, `cert_imprv_non_hstd_val`, `cert_land_hstd_val`, `cert_land_non_hstd_val`, `cert_ag_use_val`, `cert_ag_market`, `cert_market`, `cert_ag_loss`, `cert_appraised_val`, `cert_ten_percent_cap`, `cert_assessed_val`.

`prop_id` SHALL be declared as Access LONG with signed 32-bit representation and width 4. `geo_id` and `property_status` SHALL be Access TEXT with a declared positive maximum character width no greater than 255. Every year and appraisal-value field SHALL be Access NUMERIC using the approved 17-byte wrapper with explicit precision and scale metadata. Year fields SHALL have scale 0; appraisal-value fields SHALL have scale from 0 through 4; all NUMERIC precision SHALL remain within 1 through 28.

The schema descriptor SHALL declare `prop_id`, `geo_id`, `property_status`, and `curr_val_yr` non-nullable. It SHALL declare `cert_val_yr` and every current or certified appraisal-value field nullable. Column order SHALL be irrelevant. Binding SHALL use exact case-sensitive ASCII names and MUST NOT use aliases, documentation-only names, case-insensitive fallback, or ordinal positions.

Schema validation SHALL occur before row conversion. A missing required column, exact duplicate, ASCII case-fold collision, incompatible physical type, width, precision, scale, or nullability SHALL fail respectively with `missing_required_column`, `duplicate_column`, `column_name_collision`, `incompatible_physical_type`, `incompatible_width`, `incompatible_precision`, `incompatible_scale`, or `incompatible_nullability`, and the layout SHALL be classified with `unsupported_schema`.

#### Scenario: Accept reordered compatible required columns
- **WHEN** synthetic `AD_Public` metadata contains every required column with compatible descriptors in a different order
- **THEN** validation succeeds by exact column name
- **THEN** row conversion may begin without positional binding

#### Scenario: Reject a missing required column
- **WHEN** otherwise compatible `AD_Public` metadata omits `curr_val_yr`
- **THEN** validation fails before row conversion with `missing_required_column` and `unsupported_schema`

#### Scenario: Reject duplicate and colliding names
- **WHEN** metadata repeats one exact required name or contains two names that collide under ASCII case folding
- **THEN** validation fails before row conversion with `duplicate_column` or `column_name_collision` respectively
- **THEN** neither column is selected through fallback binding

#### Scenario: Reject an incompatible required descriptor
- **WHEN** `curr_val_yr` is declared with nonzero scale or `prop_id` is not a width-4 Access LONG
- **THEN** validation fails before row conversion with `incompatible_scale` or the applicable physical-type or width diagnostic
- **THEN** the layout is classified with `unsupported_schema`

#### Scenario: Reject an unexpected table
- **WHEN** observed metadata contains `AD_Public` and any additional table
- **THEN** validation fails with `unexpected_table` and `unsupported_schema`
- **THEN** no source row is converted

### Requirement: Treat the schema fingerprint as provenance

The Collin adapter SHALL compute a lowercase hexadecimal SHA-256 schema fingerprint from deterministic UTF-8 canonical JSON containing the parser contract version, exact table name, and every observed column descriptor. Each descriptor SHALL contain its exact name, physical type, width, precision, scale, and nullability, and descriptors SHALL be sorted by exact column name before serialization. Identical metadata SHALL produce identical fingerprint input bytes and fingerprints.

Required-column compatibility SHALL be evaluated independently of the fingerprint; the fingerprint MUST NOT act as an allowlist. Additional non-required column descriptors SHALL be accepted as metadata, SHALL participate in the fingerprint, and SHALL produce the deterministic non-fatal warning `extra_columns_present`. Their row values MUST NOT be requested, read, retained in typed records, or exposed as extras.

#### Scenario: Accept a metadata-only extra column
- **WHEN** compatible `AD_Public` metadata contains every required column and one additional descriptor
- **THEN** required-column validation succeeds
- **THEN** `extra_columns_present` is reported without classifying the schema as unsupported
- **THEN** the additional descriptor changes the fingerprint while its row value is never read or retained

#### Scenario: Recompute a deterministic fingerprint
- **WHEN** equivalent observed descriptors are supplied in different orders
- **THEN** canonical sorting produces identical fingerprint input bytes and the same SHA-256 fingerprint

### Requirement: Validate Collin source values exactly

`prop_id` SHALL arrive as a Python `int`; `bool` and non-integer values MUST be rejected. Accepted values SHALL be from 1 through 2,147,483,647 inclusive, SHALL be preserved as a Collin source field, and MUST NOT be declared a unique or canonical account key.

`geo_id` SHALL arrive as Access TEXT. The adapter SHALL trim only surrounding ASCII whitespace (`U+0020`, tab, carriage return, line feed, vertical tab, and form feed), require 1 through 255 remaining Unicode scalar values, preserve remaining case, punctuation, and leading zeroes, and reject NUL, any remaining C0 control character, and any non-scalar surrogate value. `geo_id` MUST NOT be equated with `prop_id`.

`property_status` SHALL be trimmed using the same surrounding ASCII-whitespace rule and SHALL then equal exactly `Preliminary`, `InProgress`, or `Certified`. It SHALL classify only the current observation family and MUST NOT relabel or suppress a certified observation.

`curr_val_yr` SHALL be non-null. `cert_val_yr` and appraisal-value fields MAY be Access null. A malformed non-null NUMERIC value MUST fail and MUST NOT become null. Every decoded year SHALL be an integral scale-0 value from 1900 through 2100 inclusive. Generic NUMERIC decoding SHALL retain the signed D1 range, while every appraisal monetary field SHALL additionally be from 0 through `10**28 - 1` inclusive.

The adapter SHALL preserve each exact `Decimal`, declared precision, declared scale, exact source column, and Collin source-native classification. It MUST NOT infer currency, silently round, convert to float, coerce the exponent, or fill a missing current or certified value from the other family.

If `cert_val_yr` is null, every certified value SHALL be null and no certified observation SHALL be emitted. Any non-null certified value SHALL require a non-null valid `cert_val_yr`; otherwise conversion SHALL fail with `inconsistent_certified_family`.

#### Scenario: Preserve valid source lexical values
- **WHEN** a synthetic row supplies an integer `prop_id`, a whitespace-padded `geo_id` with leading zeroes, an approved property status, valid years, and exact appraisal values
- **THEN** the adapter preserves the integer identifier, trimmed case-sensitive `geo_id`, approved status, exact years, and source-native `Decimal` metadata
- **THEN** neither source identifier becomes an approved account key

#### Scenario: Reject an invalid property identifier
- **WHEN** `prop_id` is `bool`, non-integer, zero, negative, or greater than 2,147,483,647
- **THEN** conversion fails deterministically with `invalid_prop_id`

#### Scenario: Reject an invalid geographic identifier
- **WHEN** `geo_id` is blank after ASCII trimming, exceeds 255 Unicode scalar values, or contains NUL, a remaining C0 control character, or a surrogate value
- **THEN** conversion fails deterministically with `invalid_geo_id`
- **THEN** the diagnostic does not contain the identifier value

#### Scenario: Reject an unsupported property status
- **WHEN** the trimmed `property_status` is not exactly `Preliminary`, `InProgress`, or `Certified`
- **THEN** conversion fails with `invalid_property_status`

#### Scenario: Reject an invalid year or monetary value
- **WHEN** a year is non-integral or outside 1900 through 2100, or an appraisal monetary value is negative or greater than `10**28 - 1`
- **THEN** conversion fails respectively with `invalid_value_year` or `invalid_monetary_value`
- **THEN** no partial logical observation is emitted

#### Scenario: Reject an inconsistent certified family
- **WHEN** `cert_val_yr` is null and any certified value is non-null
- **THEN** conversion fails with `inconsistent_certified_family`
- **THEN** the current year or values are not copied into the certified family

### Requirement: Emit separate immutable current and certified observations

The Collin adapter SHALL create adapter-local typed source records and immutable Collin observations. Every valid physical row SHALL emit exactly one current observation using `curr_val_yr`, `property_status`, and the eleven required `curr_*` appraisal-value fields. When `cert_val_yr` is non-null and at least one certified value is non-null, the same physical row SHALL emit a separate certified observation using that year and the eleven required `cert_*` fields.

`curr_market`, `curr_appraised_val`, `curr_assessed_val`, `cert_market`, `cert_appraised_val`, and `cert_assessed_val` SHALL retain their distinct source semantics. Homestead, non-homestead, land, agricultural-use, agricultural-market, agricultural-loss, and ten-percent-cap fields SHALL remain exact Collin source-native values unless a later accepted contract assigns broader semantics.

Current and certified observations SHALL retain distinct source family and year identity even when their values or years match. One family MUST NOT overwrite, merge with, suppress, relabel, deduplicate, or provide missing values for the other. `prop_id`, `geo_id`, and repeated physical rows SHALL be preserved without deduplication, owner roll-up, or approval of an account identity.

#### Scenario: Emit current and certified observations from one row
- **WHEN** a valid synthetic row has current year 2026 with `property_status` `Preliminary` and certified year 2025 with populated certified values
- **THEN** one current observation classified from `Preliminary` is emitted for 2026
- **THEN** one separate certified observation is emitted for 2025
- **THEN** each value retains its exact current or certified source column and source-native semantics
- **THEN** no value is copied between families

#### Scenario: Omit an absent certified observation
- **WHEN** `cert_val_yr` and every certified value are null
- **THEN** the valid current observation is emitted
- **THEN** no certified observation is emitted or synthesized

#### Scenario: Preserve repeated physical rows
- **WHEN** two valid synthetic rows contain the same `prop_id` and `geo_id` with different one-based physical row numbers
- **THEN** both typed source records and their observations are retained at physical source grain
- **THEN** no identifier combination is treated as a unique key and no row is deduplicated

### Requirement: Retain complete bounded provenance and diagnostics

Caller-supplied release identifier and source member SHALL be required adapter inputs and MUST NOT be inferred from row values. Every typed source record and logical observation SHALL retain the release identifier, source member, exact table name, one-based physical row number, parser contract version, schema fingerprint, source family, source year, property status, and exact source column for each value.

The diagnostic vocabulary SHALL be closed to `missing_required_table`, `unexpected_table`, `missing_required_column`, `duplicate_column`, `column_name_collision`, `incompatible_physical_type`, `incompatible_width`, `incompatible_precision`, `incompatible_scale`, `incompatible_nullability`, `unsupported_schema`, `invalid_numeric_buffer`, `invalid_numeric_sign`, `invalid_numeric_precision`, `invalid_numeric_scale`, `numeric_precision_overflow`, `negative_zero`, `invalid_prop_id`, `invalid_geo_id`, `invalid_property_status`, `invalid_value_year`, `invalid_monetary_value`, `inconsistent_certified_family`, and `extra_columns_present`.

A diagnostic MAY contain only its stable code and, when applicable, a field or table name, one-based row number, and schema fingerprint. It MUST NOT contain a complete source row, arbitrary source value, `prop_id`, `geo_id`, owner or DBA name, mailing or situs address, protected identity, credential, or host-local path.

#### Scenario: Retain observation provenance
- **WHEN** a valid synthetic physical row emits current and certified observations
- **THEN** both observations retain the caller-supplied release and member, table, row, contract version, fingerprint, family, year, status, and exact source-column lineage
- **THEN** source identity is not inferred from row data

#### Scenario: Redact a malformed value diagnostic
- **WHEN** a malformed synthetic row contains sentinel source values that are prohibited from diagnostics
- **THEN** conversion fails with the applicable closed diagnostic code and only permitted bounded metadata
- **THEN** the complete row, sentinel values, identifiers, identities, addresses, credentials, and host paths are absent

### Requirement: Keep the Collin foundation adapter-local and non-production

Implementation SHALL be limited to the existing Collin adapter module, one adapter-local Collin test module, one adapter-local synthetic fixture module, and directly related Collin source documentation. It SHALL preserve the existing county source registry contract and MUST NOT create a competing `collin/` package beside the existing `collin.py` module.

The change MUST NOT add network access, county contact, county artifacts, Access databases, archives, production rows, production credentials, an Access runtime or container, a new dependency, production acquisition, services, APIs, persistence, migrations, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Airflow, DAGs, workflows, policies, infrastructure, deployment, owner publication, protected-identity reconstruction, or a production-ready designation. It MUST NOT modify `property_tax_domain` or `property_tax_application` or infer authoritative tax-bill, payment, delinquency, penalty, or interest behavior from appraisal values.

Until Issue #43 is separately accepted and implemented, this change MUST NOT define a production release-processing API, shared vendor-neutral source-record, provenance, native-value, or bounded-streaming abstraction, or Collin DAG integration. All records and conversion types introduced here SHALL remain explicitly Collin-specific and adapter-local.

#### Scenario: Exercise the foundation without production dependencies
- **WHEN** the Collin contract tests run in a clean checkout containing only the committed synthetic fixtures
- **THEN** decoder, schema, row, observation, provenance, privacy, and diagnostic behavior is exercised without network access, an Access runtime, county data, owner data, or production credentials
- **THEN** no domain, application, service, DAG, persistence, publication, infrastructure, or deployment behavior changes

#### Scenario: Enforce the Issue #43 boundary
- **WHEN** the implementation is reviewed
- **THEN** every new record, provenance value, native value, and conversion remains Collin-specific within `property_tax_adapters`
- **THEN** no production release-processing, shared streaming, shared vendor-neutral record, or Collin DAG contract is introduced
